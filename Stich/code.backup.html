Frequently Asked Questions

You can shop directly through our global atelier checkout. We accept international payments via credit cards, Apple Pay, and Klarna. Taxes and duties are calculated upfront for full transparency with DDP (Delivered Duty Paid) shipping to over 80 countries worldwide.
We offer complimentary worldwide express shipping on all orders over $100. Standard delivery requires 2-4 business days for Western Europe and North America, and 3-6 business days for the Asia-Pacific region via DHL Express carbon-neutral courier.
We support Visa, Mastercard, American Express, Apple Pay, Google Pay, PayPal, and interest-free installment options including Klarna and Afterpay. All transactions are protected by bank-level 256-bit encryption.
To preserve artisanal integrity and avoid waste, our Limited and Atelier drops are produced in strictly numbered runs. While core Batavia Essentials are periodically re-milled, bespoke pieces rarely restock once an archive cycle closes.
We welcome returns and size exchanges within 30 days of parcel arrival. Garments must be unworn with original tags attached and returned inside the provided archival preservation box. Prepaid return slips are enclosed with every parcel.
